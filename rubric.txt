KARGO HIRING RUBRIC: PRODUCT MANAGER (PM) AND SENIOR PRODUCT MANAGER (SPM)

Source: patterns in Arjun Mehta's 5 past hires rated "Exceeds Expectations" (Rohan Desai, Sunita
Krishnamurthy, Aditya Shetty, Meghna Tiwari, Lavanya Iyer), checked against his 3 weaker hires
(Vikram Nair and Rahul Bose: Meets; Preetham Rao: Below). Not the job descriptions.
The JDs are used only for role level: PM = 2-4 yrs product experience, SPM = 5-8 yrs.

HOW TO SCORE
- Score every candidate against BOTH rubrics (PM and SPM).
- Score each criterion 0-5 using only evidence written in the CV. No evidence = 0. Do not infer.
- Criterion points = (score / 5) x weight. Total = sum of criterion points, out of 100.
- Give a one-line reason per criterion that quotes or points to the specific CV evidence.
- Pedigree is NOT a criterion: college names, MBA brand, certifications, conference talks,
  PRD templates and frameworks earn 0 points on their own. (Past hire Vikram Nair had the
  strongest pedigree and showed none of the patterns below.)

SCORE ANCHORS (apply to every criterion)
5 = Strong, specific, repeated evidence with numbers or named outcomes
3 = Clear evidence, but once, partial, or in an adjacent context
1 = Weak or indirect evidence (claimed, not shown)
0 = No evidence

==================================================================
PM RUBRIC (weights total 100)
==================================================================

1. GROUND-FLOOR OPERATIONS EXPERIENCE (25)
   Worked hands-on inside logistics operations (freight forwarder, CHA, port, 3PL, warehouse)
   before or alongside product work. Hands-on ops in an adjacent physical domain (plant floor,
   fulfilment, hospital ops) scores up to 3. Consulting, advising or building FOR ops from a
   desk scores at most 1.
   Behind it: Rohan Desai (family CHA, JNPT), Sunita Krishnamurthy (freight documentation),
   Aditya Shetty (JNPT port services), Meghna Tiwari (freight forwarder), Lavanya Iyer
   (Mahindra Logistics). 5 of 8 hires.

2. BUILDS UNASKED FIXES THAT OTHERS ADOPT (20)
   Noticed a gap nobody assigned, built a fix (tool, tracker, process, checklist), and other
   people or teams adopted it as the standard.
   Behind it: Rohan Desai (Excel tracker adopted by 12-person team in 2 weeks; weekend
   prototype used by 30 colleagues), Sunita (weekend workflow redesign kept permanently),
   Lavanya (dashboard adopted by 2 other regions), Meghna (onboarding framework now team-wide).

3. OWNS DECISIONS WITHOUT A LAYER ABOVE (20)
   Sole PM, first PM, or no PM manager above; made product calls directly rather than
   through committees, sign-off chains or a PM lead. One module inside a large PM org
   scores at most 2.
   Behind it: Lavanya ("sole PM"; "makes calls we trusted immediately. She doesn't hedge"),
   Sunita ("limited oversight"; owns processes start to finish), Aditya Shetty (runs the full
   sales cycle independently, no account manager layer).

4. KILLS WHAT DOESN'T WORK AND OWNS FAILURES (20)
   Killed or retired features based on usage data, especially their own ideas; writes
   post-mortems; openly documents what went wrong. Short cycles score higher.
   Behind it: Lavanya (killed 2 features despite strong qualitative demand; wrote the outage
   post-mortem herself), Aditya Shetty (post-mortem on a lost deal, now standard practice).

5. DISCOVERY IN THE FIELD, NOT FROM A DESK (15)
   Spent real time embedded with users where the work happens (site visits, weeks on the
   floor, sitting with ops teams). Discovery only via account managers, workshops, analytics
   or calls scores at most 2.
   Behind it: Rohan Desai (works directly with freight ops teams, no product layer),
   Aditya Shetty (worked alongside terminal ops at peak), Lavanya (parallel discovery tracks
   with forwarders that uncovered a workflow mismatch).

==================================================================
SPM RUBRIC (weights total 100)
==================================================================

1. OWNS CONSEQUENTIAL CALLS WITH NO COMMITTEE (25)
   Owned a product area or function with no senior PM layer above; made hard, long-term
   calls (architecture, build vs buy, what not to build) and lived with them. Decisions
   reviewed by HQ, governance committees or client sign-off score at most 1.
   Behind it: Lavanya ("doesn't hedge"), Aditya Shetty (runs the full cycle independently),
   Sunita (owns end to end with limited oversight), Rohan Desai ("most senior engineer in
   the room"; translates field needs into spec with no product layer).

2. GROUND-FLOOR OPERATIONS EXPERIENCE (20)
   Same definition as PM criterion 1.
   Behind it: Rohan Desai, Sunita, Aditya Shetty, Meghna, Lavanya.

3. KILLS OWN IDEAS AND OWNS FAILURES (20)
   Same definition as PM criterion 4. Extra credit for killing their own idea or absorbing
   sunk cost to make the call.
   Behind it: Lavanya, Aditya Shetty.

4. SETS STANDARDS OTHERS ADOPT (20)
   Built practices, processes or tools that became the team's or company's standard;
   raised others (mentored people who were promoted, created discovery or review practices).
   Behind it: Rohan Desai (introduced code review and on-call; cut P1 incidents 40%;
   mentee promoted in 14 months), Meghna (30-60-90 framework adopted by whole CS team),
   Sunita (SOPs adopted in full; trainee promoted to team lead), Aditya (loss post-mortem
   became standard practice).

5. INTEGRATION AND DATA DEPTH FROM REAL USE (15)
   Shipped integrations or data-layer work (carrier systems, customs/port portals, ERPs,
   freight tools) informed by knowing what the data really contains in practice.
   Behind it: Rohan Desai (BoL verification module; legacy data vendor migration with no
   data loss), Sunita (led paper-to-cloud FMS migration), Lavanya (first API documentation;
   cut integration time by 2 weeks). Note: Preetham Rao (Below Expectations) also built
   integrations, but only from inside a large team with no field exposure. Technical
   skill without ground-floor context does not score above 2.

==================================================================
RED FLAGS (do not score; list them in the brief for the human reviewer)
==================================================================
- Years of product experience outside the role range (PM 2-4, SPM 5-8)
- Two CVs with near-identical text under different names
- Contact links or URLs that do not match the candidate's name
- Claims with no specifics (no numbers, no named outcome) repeated across the CV
